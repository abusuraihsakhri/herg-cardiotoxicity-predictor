hERG-Guard

Transparent, local rule-based audit of generic numeric inputs

Source on GitHub ↗

Not a cardiotoxicity predictor. This prototype does not calculate hERG IC50, assess QTc, analyze SMILES, or provide clinically validated risk estimates. The arbitrary input thresholds are software demonstration rules, not ICH S7B/E14 safety limits. Do not use for patient care or compound selection.

Evaluate one record

Exact demonstration rules

Primary metric > 25: elevated. Secondary metric > 12: elevated. Manual critical flag: critical. Descriptor containing DISCORDANT, ANOMALY, MUTANT, VIOLATION, FAIL, or REJECT: elevated. Highest tier determines the overall outcome.

All computation runs in this browser. No information is uploaded, persisted, signed, or transmitted to an API. Avoid entering patient-identifiable data.
